## Exotic Launch Timeline

Kalshi rolled out exotics product-by-product over ~6 months. Every new family launched alongside a major sporting or cultural event.

| Launch date | Series | Trigger event |
|---|---|---|
| **Sep 17, 2025** | `KXMVENFLMULTIGAME` | **First exotic ever** — NFL season opener |
| Sep 28, 2025 | `KXMVENFLSINGLEGAME` | Single-game NFL parlays |
| Oct 3, 2025 | `KXMVENFLMULTIGAMEEXTENDED` | Extended NFL product |
| Nov 13, 2025 | `KXMVENBAMULTIGAMEEXTENDED` + `KXMVEMENTIONSSINGLE` | NBA season tip-off |
| Nov 18, 2025 | `KXMVESPORTSMULTIGAMEEXTENDED` + `KXMVENBASINGLEGAME` | Cross-sport multi-game (now the largest series by volume) |
| Jan 29, 2026 | `KXMVEGRAMMYS` | Awards season |
| Feb 27, 2026 | `KXMVECROSSCATEGORY` + `KXMVEOSCARS` | Cross-category product + Oscars |
| Mar 16, 2026 | `KXMVECBCHAMPIONSHIP` | March Madness |

**Pattern:** Kalshi times new exotic launches to expected demand spikes. The
NFL was the testbed (3 products in 3 weeks); the biggest revenue product
(`KXMVESPORTSMULTIGAMEEXTENDED`) launched alongside NBA season; the most
recent additions cluster around awards season and tournament play.

*Source: first observed `daily_volume > 0` per series in `kalshi.market_report`,
verified May 16, 2026.*
